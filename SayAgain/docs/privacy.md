# SayAgain — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 September 2026_

SayAgain performs speech recognition and translation entirely on your iPhone.

- **We do not collect any data.** SayAgain has no account system and no server. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
- **We do not transmit audio, transcripts, or translations.** All processing happens on-device using Apple's Speech Recognition and Translation frameworks. If your iPhone is in Airplane Mode, SayAgain still works.
- **We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.** No Firebase. No Google Analytics. No Facebook SDK. None.
- **We do not share information with anyone**, because we do not have any information to share.

## What SayAgain accesses

- **Microphone** — required to record your voice. Access is granted by you the first time you tap Record and can be revoked in iOS Settings at any time. Audio is processed locally and discarded when you close a session unless you explicitly export a transcript.
- **File system (Documents folder)** — SayAgain writes transcripts and translations to your app's local Documents folder so you can view, share, or export them. These files remain on your device unless you choose to share them.
